This project demonstrates reconstruction parity, not operational parity. The published artifacts are conceptual architectures, normalization adapters, and synthetic demonstrations. 01 · the threat model

Targets assume that rotating talkgroups, jumping between frequencies, or separating communications across channels (P25 trunked radio, Meshtastic LoRa, BLE beacons, WiFi APs, cellular LTE) preserves operational pseudonymity. deanon-demo shows that third-order statistical inference destroys this assumption:

  • Frequency Association: Recurring co-presence on band X during temporal window T.
  • Temporal Fingerprinting: Radio keying duration, transmission bursts, shift change schedules, and cadence clustering.
  • Social Graph Extraction: Unmasking who coordinates with whom by calculating spatial co-presence across transitions.
  • Metadata Correlation: Radio unit IDs, encryption state changes, and sequential call flows.

Together, this reconstructs fleet memberships, operational tempo, mission types, and movement tracks—all without decrypting a single voice packet or payload.

02 · architecture: mirkwood master fusion engine

Mirkwood functions as the master cross-channel fusion engine. While individual sensors operate independently (from USB serial scanners to wardriving rigs), Mirkwood ingests their telemetry through dedicated Python adapters into a single normalized table: emission_events.

04 · evidentiary standing & the legal cascade

As documented in EVIDENTIARY.md, treating a correlation lock as an identification destroys legal cases instead of building them:

The One Controlling Fact

A correlation lock is a co-presence hypothesis, not an identification.

It means two pseudonyms on two channels shared a fingerprint or a spatiotemporal window. It is a lead to begin an independent investigation, never the proof.

The Daubert Reliability Failure

If correlation locks are used to justify a search warrant or arrest:

  1. Error Rate: In any dense environment, the system emits ~125 false locks per window. An identification technique where most outputs are coincidental fails Daubert reliability.
  2. Falsifiability & Validation: The published methodology runs strictly on synthetic lab data. It has never touched ground-truth field data.
  3. Fruit of the Poisonous Tree: When a judge suppresses the warrant predicate, all real downstream physical evidence is suppressed along with it.
  4. Forgeability: Because identifiers (BLE MACs, P25 unit IDs, SIP URIs) can be emitted and spoofed in software, anyone with a transmitter can manufacture a fake lock.
